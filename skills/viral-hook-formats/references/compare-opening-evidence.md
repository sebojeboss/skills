# Compare opening evidence

## When to read

Use this when selecting examples by results, explaining a reported drop, comparing versions or proposing a bounded hook test. It is not needed for every copy edit.

## How to do the work

Define what is being compared: published post, ad ID, media version, opening segment or common-body variant. Keep source identity and capture date. Two files with identical bytes can be different publications; similar wording does not establish the same creative. Distinguish missing data from zero and a planned version from an exported, published or measured one.

For numeric performance, compare within the same account and retain platform, post age, observation window, duration, audience/distribution conditions and other changes. Cross-account examples can teach qualitative craft; raw views do not rank their hook effectiveness. A reference selected for personal taste need not be an outlier. A high-view example is a place to investigate, not proof that every prop or line was necessary.

Read ordinary and weaker counterparts when they could challenge the explanation. If a lower-reach version delivers the first useful item sooner, that is evidence against “earlier fulfillment alone explains the higher total.” It is not evidence that delaying fulfillment is always better. A useful diagnosis can remain unresolved.

Recover the metric definition before calculating:

- **Hook rate** may refer to different early-view thresholds and denominators. Record the actual formula and window.
- **Hold rate** needs both the retained-view threshold and its starting population.
- **Skip or stayed-to-watch measures** may use feed opportunities, while another percentage includes only people who stayed.
- **Average percentage viewed** depends on video duration and eligible views. Changing length can change the percentage without improving the same viewing experience.

Use current authoritative platform definitions when a task depends on them. Do not promote a creator's historical benchmark into a platform rule. If the denominator is unknown, label the metric and keep the comparison qualitative until the missing definition is obtained.

Separate outcomes. Attention does not establish brand recall. Comments requesting a resource do not establish delivery, use or qualified demand. A purchase count does not establish contribution without costs and attribution context. Do not estimate commercial success from ad longevity or organic popularity.

When proposing a comparison, state the specific craft question and what is held constant. Two openings using the same body can investigate the invitation if audience, delivery and measurement support that comparison. If one opening changes the buying reason and demands a different demonstration, treat it as a concept comparison. Do not claim isolated hook causality after changing topic, footage, runtime, voice, distribution and offer together.

For a few records, use a simple table or exact arithmetic. No scoring framework is required. A small Markdown table can preserve the common body, opening version, reason for change, required evidence and observed outcome. Meta allocation, experiment delivery and attribution decisions belong with campaign management.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative; the numbers are invented to show the reasoning.

An account's two posts use similar openings. One has more plays, but it is older; the other reaches the first useful item earlier. The defensible reading is that early fulfillment does not by itself explain the observed totals. Compare the actual continuations and note the unmatched age rather than naming a winning sentence. A later matched observation may narrow the uncertainty but still does not isolate all causes.

A supplied export labels both columns “hook rate.” Version A has 400 early views out of 1,000 impressions, or 40%. Version B has 240 longer views out of 400 early views, or 60%. The arithmetic is valid, but the ranking is not: these rates describe different stages and populations. Recover matching measures before selecting a winner. Do not subtract them to claim a 20-point gain.

A third revision removes an abstract opening and also rebuilds the body with demonstrations. Better results, if later documented, belong to the changed execution. Preserve the changes rather than crediting only the line the editor most remembers. The practical next question may concern the whole execution, not a demand to manufacture an isolated test.

## Deliverable and checks

State the supported conclusion and any uncertainty that changes the recommendation. Include a competing explanation or contradictory example when the available evidence supplies one; name a missing comparison only if it could distinguish the explanations. Label unrun tests as proposed. Keep an effective opening selected while investigating uncertainty.
