# Read and prepare captions

## When to read

Read when public captions or supplied subtitles need searchable wording, timing or comparison. A short supplied sentence needs no parsing workflow. Captions establish recorded wording at their inspected fidelity, not gestures, performance, music or cuts.

## Preserve the source sequence

Use supplied text, the source's supported transcript interface, or an available subtitle editor/local text tool. Keep the original VTT, SRT or transcript unchanged. When selecting an excerpt, retain its cue numbers or source lines, original timestamps and neighboring cues needed to understand it. Untimed text stays untimed; do not invent a first-two-seconds claim from it.

Normalize spacing only when it improves reading. Keep raw wording and the mapping back to it. Subtitle markup is not necessarily spoken text. Rolling captions repeat prior words, but intentional spoken repetition can look similar: inspect adjacent cues before making a reading-only cleanup. Do not automatically deduplicate every repeated phrase or discard overlapping intervals.

Check that start/end times are present, ordered and expressed in the right units. Flag malformed timing or partial retrieval explicitly. For a large file, use an existing subtitle tool or a small local transformation and compare representative outputs against the original. Successful parsing cannot establish that an auto-caption heard the product name or price correctly.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: cue 1 reads “Watch this” at 0–2 seconds and cue 2 reads “Watch this fold” at 1–3 seconds. Preserve both source cues; their overlap suggests rolling captions. A later “Watch this” at 8–9 seconds may be deliberate repetition. A cleaned reading excerpt can combine the first pair while retaining where each word came from.

Keep an opening and a later payoff as separate inspected portions. Do not stretch the opening interval to include proof that appears afterward. Verify consequential claims against original audio or another authoritative product source where available; otherwise state the uncertainty.

## Deliver the useful excerpt

Return the relevant wording, timing, source locator and inspection limit. A small Markdown excerpt or comparison table is enough. Missing retrieval is missing evidence, not permission to reconstruct a transcript. Preparing captions does not establish audiovisual review or permission to reuse the footage.

# Record public observations

Use this when comparing or reusing findings. For a direct answer, a source link and the relevant distinction can suffice; use existing notes or research formats.

## Preserve what makes the finding comparable

Attach the source URL, precise locator, capture date, entity and actual inspection coverage to each consequential finding. Include country, locale, filters and access limits when they affect comparison. Separate the source's event date from observation time; leave unknown timezone or precision unknown.

Distinguish direct observations, reported accounts and your inferences. Give an inference its supporting observations and competing explanation. Complete capture within a stated scope does not settle interpretation; blocked access does not establish absence.

Scope identity to the provider and entity: a Page is not an ad account, an ad ID is not a creative, and matching wording does not establish identity. If an ID is unavailable, use a descriptive local label and state the gap. Preserve relevant versions, raw values, currency and units. Unknown cost is not zero.

## Compare the evidence before the counts

Illustrative: yesterday's capture covered active image ads in Canada; today's covers all media in Australia. A newly visible ad may reflect the changed scope. Even matching filters can yield unequal coverage because of scrolling or access. Report “not reobserved” unless evidence establishes that spending stopped.

An advertised guarantee records the promise on that date. A buyer's refund account reports one episode; it neither replaces the historical policy nor establishes that every refund succeeds. Retain both and identify any inferred relationship.

Keep quotations distinct from interpretation and check consequential claims against the correct source and version. Record structure does not certify truth, completeness or causality.

Capture only needed evidence. Keep private records in approved storage; cleaned notes are not necessarily anonymized. A public source does not authorize sending the user's brief, intent or linked private findings to another service.
