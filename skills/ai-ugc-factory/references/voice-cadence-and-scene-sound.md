# Voice, cadence and scene sound

## When to read

Use when speech contains wrong words, pronunciation fails, delivery sounds read or robotic, voice identity changes, or audio does not belong to the pictured scene.

## How to do the work

Start with a timestamp and intended effect. “Sounds AI” is a report to investigate, not a diagnosis. Separate the following questions before choosing an intervention:

| Question | Useful evidence | Intervention to consider |
|---|---|---|
| Did it say the intended words/numbers? | Intended text, heard output, transcript uncertainty | Correct source text or take; regenerate/record the affected thought |
| Is the pronunciation correct here? | Desired sound from an appropriate source and full sentence | Audition alias/respelling or a guide performance |
| Is the speaker character suitable and stable? | Same sentence with intended guide and target voice | Cast against the real guide, change target or retain a selected take |
| Does the phrase express the right thought? | Stress, pause, pitch and rhythm in context | Change delivery/phrasing, not only global speed |
| Is there noise or incompatible recording texture? | Dry original and adjacent shots at comparable loudness | Re-record, repair the actual noise or adjust treatment conservatively |
| Do lips and gestures belong with the words? | Continuous audiovisual playback | Revise performance/render; audio quality alone is insufficient |

Keep written spelling separate from a pronunciation alias. A useful alias can sound wrong when embedded in a longer sentence, so audition the actual line. A personal name may have more than one legitimate pronunciation; a dictionary guess does not outrank the person's stated pronunciation. Provider-specific phoneme markup and text controls need current support verification. Do not infer universal syntax from one appealing output.

For cadence, locate the thought boundary and contrast. A long silence can feel hesitant even when it matches a timing target. Shortening every pause can remove confidence, humor or processing time. Try a changed guide delivery or sentence structure when the attitude is wrong; global speed changes all words and cannot independently fix stress. Preserve fixed wording where required and identify any meaning change rather than calling it mere punctuation.

Voice conversion uses a guide performance, but the guide can affect perceived identity as well as expression. Audition with the intended speaker and delivery range. A library preview is not the converted result. Ordinary narration succeeding does not prove shouting or singing will work. When a fresh take is chosen, retain its actual bytes and source record; do not silently synthesize another take for video production.

Check speech content before polishing. ASR can flag omissions or substitutions, but it can also mishear numbers, contractions and names. Listen to the disputed span or mark it unresolved. Correcting captions to the intended script while leaving the wrong spoken number conceals the defect. Treat pronunciation correctness, intelligibility and expressive fit as separate judgments.

Preserve the dry master. Match adjacent shots for perceived level before deciding one recording is better. Loudness measurements help reveal differences; they do not certify pleasantness. A close visible microphone and a distant room scene can support different recording expectations, but do not apply a universal reverb preset. Compare the treatment in its actual scene. Added room sound cannot fix missing words; cleanup can damage consonants and breaths, so recheck the cleaned output.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative scenario: a product name sounds right in isolation but takes the wrong stress in a sentence. Keep the same voice and sentence while comparing an explicitly approved pronunciation alias and a guide recording. A different voice may pronounce it correctly but lose the selected character; that is a real tradeoff, not a free repair. Accept only the actual contextual result, not the alias text.

Illustrative edit: direct-to-camera audio is quiet while the voiceover insert is sharp and loud. First compare level and recording distance, then determine whether gain matching is enough. If the tone remains incompatible, a matching pickup may be better than a music bed that masks the seam. Music must not obscure intelligibility or substitute for fixing a conspicuous mismatch.

## Deliverable and checks

Return the exact span, failure class, preserved inputs, proposed repair and actual inspected result. Keep technical decode/transcript checks distinct from listening and continuous lip/gesture review. Record residual defects rather than declaring the voice ID fixed forever.
