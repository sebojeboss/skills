# Deadlines, staging and submission

## When to read

Use for live response timing, withdrawal, editing submitted evidence, a stale snapshot or a request to send.

## How to do the work

Record the actual case cutoff with timezone, source and observation time. Separate that from general network windows and your own preparation target. A date without timezone remains uncertain. If the deadline is close, prioritize the available truthful response and the specific missing fact; do not wait blindly for a better carrier event. A late good-faith forwarding route is not a guaranteed extension.

Keep local draft, provider-uploaded file, staged evidence, submitted response, under review and decision as distinct observations. Preserve the provider's raw state and stage. Some update operations submit unless a staging parameter is explicit; read the current contract before using them. An initial evidence lock does not prove every later appeal is unavailable, and later appeal availability does not make the original packet editable.

A customer's withdrawal request is evidence of an intention. It does not itself establish issuer acceptance, faster resolution or returned funds. Keep meeting the actual case obligations until the provider's state establishes otherwise. An inquiry escalation may require a new response even if the earlier inquiry had documents.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: a customer emails that they withdrew the claim, but the portal still says a response is due tomorrow. Include the communication where relevant and prepare the response; do not close the task as won. If a later appeal becomes available, preserve the original deadline history and record the new stage's cutoff rather than treating the change as an extension of the old submission.

## Deliverable and checks

Keep a small state note: observed stage/time, due time/source, last confirmed receipt, next action, owner and unresolved state. Nothing watches the case for you; set the next check explicitly. Before an authorized send, compare case/account, packet version, fields and current cutoff; afterward preserve receipt and read back status. A lost response requires reconciliation before retry. Carry forward existing authorization; ask only for a genuinely missing consequential permission after preparing the exact operation.
