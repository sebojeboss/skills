# Quality, description and return evidence

## When to read

Use for wrong goods, dimensions, defects, damage, missing parts or disagreement over return conditions.

## How to do the work

Recover the offer as it existed at purchase: selected variant, dimensions, material, included components, disclosed limitations and any approved customization. A current product page may have changed. Compare those agreed facts with the specific reported defect and available inspection evidence. Organize by disputed characteristic, not by all the good things about the product.

Separate dispatch condition, transit damage and condition after use. Identify when photos were taken and what connects them to this order. If identity is disputed, use supported serial/lot/order records; a similar-looking item is not a proven substitution. Preserve the customer's objection and any evidence favoring it.

Build the return sequence: complaint, proposed remedy, conditions, labels/instructions, buyer response, receipt, inspection, refund promise and later revision. A prepaid label establishes an offered return route, not that the buyer received conforming goods or that every refund condition is enforceable. Keep the original refund commitment even if inspection changed the merchant's position; explain the change with contemporaneous facts. Do not invent a policy retrospectively.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: a buyer expected a shelf to fit a recess. The selected listing shows its external dimensions, but a drawing elsewhere shows internal dimensions. The merchant's measurement alone does not resolve ambiguous presentation. Compare the purchase-time material and acknowledge the ambiguity. A return offer may be commercially sensible even when the product matches one dimension table.

Contrast a clearly selected small variant with a received large variant confirmed by its label. Delivery and a no-returns statement do not rebut the wrong-item record. Prepare an accurate concession/remedy recommendation rather than a persuasive but irrelevant defense.

## Deliverable and checks

Provide an agreed-versus-observed table, exhibit locators and return chronology. Distinguish evidence of agreement from evidence of performance. Return handling belongs with fulfillment/support; actual refunds and messages remain scoped operations. Appeal preparation uses [appeals](appeals-and-concession-decisions.md), not an assumption that refusing a return guarantees recovery.
