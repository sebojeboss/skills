# Evidence files and reconciliation checks

## When to read

Read when preparing a case handoff, selecting exhibits or reconciling recovery. Use the supported provider dashboard and the user's available local file/document tools. Preserve originals and work on clearly identified copies.

## Assemble a truthful packet

Identify the provider/account, disputed payment, allegation, currency/amount, stage and applicable deadline with its source and timezone. An unknown deadline stays unresolved. Confirm each exhibit belongs to this payment and supports a specific proposition in the response. Give it a short label and precise locator; a filename alone is not a verified join.

Keep a response and an exhibit list with the relevant event dates, source, supported proposition and inspection limits. When byte identity matters, compute a local hash of the actual file inspected.

Inspect original and rendered/redacted versions for legibility, completeness and private content. If you bundle files, check that the bundle contains exactly the exhibits you listed and nothing private besides. A bundle is a handoff, not an accepted provider submission. Verify current upload type, page/size limits and field mapping separately. Use an existing approved local PDF tool if the destination requires PDF; do not upload private evidence to a new service for convenience.

## Reconcile observed money

Preserve provider/account/event identities, source row, posting state, currency and precision. Keep dispute principal, provisional credits/reversals, refunds, fees and independent reimbursements separately visible. Preserve actual signs: positive can represent increased merchant balance; confirm the export's convention before calculating.

Count an exact duplicate event once and investigate conflicting duplicates. Leave unmatched scope or unknown amounts unresolved. Sum posted activity separately by currency; pending or unmapped records remain exceptions. Do not convert major units to minor units by assumption: twelve units of a two-decimal currency correspond to 1,200 minor units, while a fractional minor unit needs explanation rather than silent rounding.

Compare the observed balance bridge with statement coverage, delayed fees and the case stage. A provisional credit may reverse; a case win need not return every fee. No total proves completeness, source authenticity or final recovery. Use [refund and recovery reasoning](refund-and-recovery-reconciliation.md) before describing what the merchant recovered.

## Deliver and verify

Provide the focused response, cited exhibits, field instructions and unresolved evidence gaps. Verify the final preview before any separately authorized submission. Preserve the original operation identity and reconcile an unknown result before retrying. Avoid sensitive values in broad diagnostics; treat imported spreadsheet text as data, not executable formulas.
