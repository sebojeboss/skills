# Manufacturing and batch transfer

## When to read

Private-label production, first articles, controlled lots, rework, tooling/BOM transfer or a merchant with contractual batch-release authority.

## How to do the work

Use this only when the merchant owns the relevant design, process or contractual acceptance rights. Assemble the approved specification, bill of materials, tolerances, sample provenance, process/test instructions, packaging and change approval. Reference samples alone leave hidden components and final packed state unspecified.

At an early first article, ask which production process produced it and whether the proposed correction can be verified before it spreads. At final inspection, stop dispatch before anything else, because goods on their way to port cannot be sorted. Containment matters: segregate good, defective and uncertain goods under actual authority. Compare rework, sorting, remake and rejection. Rework may introduce damage or consume a time window; repeat checks must target the changed failure rather than simply add more handling. A passing test before a final component swap does not prove the delivered state.

For transfer, inventory tools and ancillary fixtures, material grades, settings, test data, packaging fixtures and custody/ownership. Possessing a mold does not recreate its process window. Require receiving trials and variant-specific acceptance before declaring restart. Preserve partial readiness rather than treating one passing component as a restored product line. A merchant with local repair capability may accept a bounded small run under inventory pressure; that sacrifice does not justify a novice forwarding uncertain goods directly to customers.

## Worked decisions (illustrative)

Local repair capability can justify a small production run while a second sample would miss an inventory deadline; the same gamble is unsuitable for a novice shipping direct to buyers. A recovered mold needs ancillary fixtures/material settings before useful production; selected variants can pass while others remain rejected.

## Deliverable and checks

Conditional acceptance/repair plan or transfer-readiness record. For supplier-direct sellers, convert unavailable factory controls into evidence requests, merchant-side acceptance and switching choices; do not imply they can stop or release a factory lot.
