# Distribution and affiliate economics

## When to read

Read when setting up affiliate distribution, judging whether commissions or network reach pay, or investigating missing sale credit or Blueprint royalties.

## How to do the work

Identify whether the job concerns a merchant product affiliate, a business-referral partner or a Blueprint publisher before reading a commission field. Calculate contribution after the proposed commission and expected refunds, check eligible surfaces and exclusions, then prepare the selected supported configuration. To diagnose missing credit, trace the qualifying sale/relationship and its precedence rules; network access or an enrolled promoter alone does not prove attributed incremental sales.

Whop's affiliate network joins eligible offers, referral tracking and commission administration. Start with a product a promoter can explain and a commission the margin can carry. Use a supported custom deal when a specific creator fits better than open participation; enabling a rate does not recruit them. Check the exact basket before promising payment: a retained affiliate code can coexist with a multi-item purchase that earns no buyer-affiliate commission. Keep working Shopify Collabs relationships when they already reach the right buyers.

Read the commission base and duration before the rate: a share of gross for every renewal is a different cost from a one-time share of the first sale. Blueprint build and publishing mechanics belong to store building, not here. Do not prescribe outreach or switch on default commissions without the user's selected action.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: an item yields 12 currency units before affiliate commission. Paying 10 per attributed sale leaves 2 before any additional refund/support burden. A percentage that looks modest relative to revenue can consume most contribution. Model refunded and ineligible sales using the actual commission contract; network enrollment alone is not proof of incremental demand. An established Shopify merchant may retain Collabs when that operating relationship meets the job.

Keep product-affiliate sale attribution, business partner attribution and Blueprint publisher royalties separate. Current Create Payment documentation describes a buyer-sale affiliate username, precedence for certain promo attribution and a multi-variant purchase exclusion. Do not put the pack business code into that field. Blueprint royalties on qualifying Whop profit are not a percentage of GMV or proof of promoter earnings. To investigate credit, trace the eligible relationship, qualifying sale, precedence and resulting ledger entry; do not infer credit because a link contains a code.
