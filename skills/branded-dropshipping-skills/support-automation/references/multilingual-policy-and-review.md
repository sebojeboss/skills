# Multilingual policy and review

## When to read

Use for translated replies, multilingual routing or market expansion. A simple translation of an already approved response need not trigger a full launch review.

## How to do the work

Identify the customer’s language preference separately from market, purchase channel and governing policy. Translation does not make a different market’s policy applicable. Retain the original message and proposed translation in approved local storage so reviewers can compare meaning rather than paraphrases of paraphrases.

First settle the underlying decision in clear language. Then translate, retaining amounts/currencies, quantities, dates, negations, conditions and the precise difference between “may,” “will,” “submitted” and “completed.” Compare the complete exchange: pronouns, item references and earlier promises can make a fluent individual turn wrong. Back-translation can flag ambiguity but is not independent proof of fidelity.

Route consequential ambiguity to a qualified language reviewer with the policy and intended outcome, not just isolated text. Correct both the reusable source answer and translated variants when the underlying policy changes. Keep technical jargon out of the buyer response; retain necessary operational terms in the internal note.

Choose staffing from actual demand and pickup ability: native-language teams, translation-assisted overflow or a language-specific review queue. Overflow coverage does not establish round-the-clock expertise. Expand enabled intents only where the same authority and remedy paths exist in that market. External translation services require approved dataflow for the actual message content; using an existing account does not authorize uploading private transcripts elsewhere.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: “You may keep the item if the return team approves the exception” loses its condition in translation. Fix the permission first; polishing the friendly tone cannot repair it. If approval is absent, preserve the conditional or route the exception.

Illustrative: a buyer writes in French while shopping from a market with a different return policy. Select policy from the transaction and applicable merchant terms, not the detected language. If the market is unresolved, request the minimum missing fact instead of confidently selecting a locale.

## Deliverable and checks

Provide the approved meaning, translated draft, reviewer questions and applicable policy/version. Distinguish language QA from executed remedy and customer-confirmed resolution. Use [pickup](notification-and-human-pickup.md) if review needs a person; do not label translated backlog as resolved work.
