# Versions, acceptance and handoff

## When to read

Use for delegated edits, multiple variants, approval, retained versions or delivery reconciliation. For a small change, a short version note is sufficient.

## How to do the work

For multi-version work, connect each source to its retained region or interval, shot, timeline occurrence and export. One take can feed several cuts; a composite can use several takes. Keep source/master, candidate, selected export and reviewer acceptance distinct. Use stable local IDs, adding hashes when exact byte identity matters.

Record who accepted which version, for what purpose, and any outstanding limitations. An agent's selection is not automatically client approval. A new render does not replace the retained one without the actual selection decision. Failed alternatives remain useful evidence for what not to repeat.

For an editor handoff, supply selected words, asset roles, timing/assembly intent and fixed versus open choices. Include fonts/linked assets, working master and intended export contract. Leave room for creative judgment where it was actually delegated. Don't describe a flat PNG as an editable design or a silent clip as a finished narrated ad.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: square and vertical exports share a master. Approval of the square version does not prove the vertical crop kept the label intact. Revise only the affected crop, inspect its final output, and attach acceptance to that file. If a later generation has finer texture but breaks the ending, retain the previous selected take and describe the challenger separately.

## Deliverable and checks

For a multi-version handoff, retain a small selection note or use the existing asset system: source version, relevant interval, candidate/export, requested change, review decision and accepted use. Verify linked files exist, intervals are in range and final exports correspond to the brief. Use hashes when byte identity matters. Exported, posted, paid, approved and commercially effective are separate states; claim only the ones evidenced.
