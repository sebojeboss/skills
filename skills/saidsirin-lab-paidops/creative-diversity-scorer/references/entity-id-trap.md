# The Entity-ID trap: what counts as one concept

> Platform mechanics change — treat the framing below as the operating model behind this skill,
> and re-verify specifics against current platform docs before quoting them as fact.

## The idea
Creative-led delivery systems retrieve and rank *creative*, and they recognize near-duplicates.
Ten crops, aspect ratios, or text tweaks of the same underlying idea are treated as, effectively,
**one entity** — not ten distinct tests. So an account with "40 ads" might present only **4 ideas**
to the auction. Diversity that matters is measured in **concepts**, not ad rows.

## What counts as a distinct concept
A concept is a distinct **idea × angle**, not a distinct file. Ask: if both ran, would the system
see two genuinely different creatives, or one with a tweak?

- **Same concept (counts as one):** same hook + same visual, different aspect ratio; a color
  change; a swapped headline; a 15s vs 30s cut of the same footage.
- **Distinct concepts (count separately):** different hook, different visual language, different
  problem/proof/offer/objection angle, different creator/format entirely (UGC testimonial vs
  studio product demo vs founder-to-camera).

## How to label for this skill
Give each creative a `concept` label that groups true variations together, plus `format`
(video/static/carousel/UGC) and `angle` (problem/proof/offer/objection, or your own taxonomy).
The scorer counts distinct concepts and coverage from those labels.

## Rules of thumb
- Aim for **~10–20 genuinely distinct concepts** in an active creative-led account; fewer than that
  is usually the real reason "creative won't scale."
- **Variations still have a job** — they help within a winning concept — but don't mistake them for
  diversity.
- Widen **angles and formats**, not just visuals: a new angle opens new demand; a new crop does not.
