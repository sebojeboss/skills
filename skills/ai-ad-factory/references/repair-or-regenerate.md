# Repair or regenerate

## When to read

Use for a defective output, repeated retries, salvage requests or revisions whose scope is unclear.

## How to do the work

Name the defect at its origin: argument, factual claim, capture, conditioning, performance, integration or export. Fixing the wrong stage wastes effort. Sharpening cannot correct speech; a plausible label cannot substantiate a claim; a new generation cannot fix an incorrectly applied trim in the exporter.

Compare the smallest viable interventions: edit a local region, reuse a clean interval, replace audio, reshoot, regenerate one scene, change the concept or omit the scene. Estimate remaining effort and risk to already correct parts. Historical spend is sunk; it does not by itself justify another retry. A local repair can introduce a sliding mask or inconsistent shadow, so inspect its relationship with surrounding frames.

When choosing among generated options, judge parts, not whole outputs. The first three seconds of one take and the closing frame of another are often the usable material; so are the first half of one line and the ending of another. A shorter rewrite can lose the plain wording that made the longer one easy to follow. Say why the pick won: "the hand stays on the handle through the lock" teaches the next attempt something; "this one looks best" doesn't.

Keep **selects**—usable material retained for the editor—even when a take fails overall. Coverage can be deliberate rather than waste. Several failed takes may contribute regions to one good composite, but that does not make them accepted outputs. Record what was retained and why.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: a bottle's label is wrong but its shape and handling are stable. Verified label art and a tracked composite may save the shot. If the hand passes through the bottle, replacing lettering leaves the decisive defect unchanged. Use a different take, different framing or real action footage. If tracking the label slips, repair the composite or abandon it; do not judge only its best frame.

A client asking for a different headline may need an editorial revision. Asking for a new product use scenario may require directorial work and new assets. Clarify the actual change rather than pricing both as one indistinguishable revision round.

## Deliverable and checks

Give the repair, preserved invariants, changed regions/intervals, sacrifice and next check. Preserve raw source and the retained version. Reinspect affected boundaries after repair and leave unaffected work alone. Stop when the deliverable meets its agreed purpose; neither compulsory reroll counts nor endless polish are universal craft rules.
