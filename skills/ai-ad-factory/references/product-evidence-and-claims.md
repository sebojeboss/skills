# Product evidence and claims

## When to read

Use when the ad depicts product action, performance, comparisons, reviews, customer experience or a mechanism. Generated brand imports and plausible mockups also need this check.

## How to do the work

List what the final audience would infer from words **and pictures**. Match each consequential statement to an authoritative product record, genuine demonstration or properly scoped customer evidence. Keep a compact table: claim, source, scope, scene/copy using it, unresolved question. The table can be a few lines; it need not become a separate dossier.

Check variant identity, package art, dimensions and actual operation independently. Correct lettering does not prove correct geometry. A realistic mechanism illustration does not prove efficacy. A real person delivering invented personal experience is still a claim problem; changing to an avatar does not solve it.

Use verified art for labels and exact text in post. When imported tool context blends products, discard the contaminated claim rather than styling it to look credible. Reconcile price, quantity, offer and landing destination. Never manufacture reviews, ratings, news coverage or customer counts to fill a visual slot.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: an umbrella photographed beside splashing water can establish appearance. It cannot establish a wind-resistance rating. If there is a genuine test with defined conditions, show what it actually demonstrates and keep those conditions consistent with the words. A cut that removes the failure point cannot make that footage a successful test.

A prototype animation can explain a proposed mechanism when labeled and framed as an illustration. A patent drawing or client approval does not establish manufactured performance. Conversely, purely decorative imagery need not be subjected to an imaginary scientific test when it makes no functional claim.

## Deliverable and checks

Repair the delivered copy/scene itself, not just a disclaimer in the chat. Remove, narrow or hold unsupported assertions. Identify whether a source establishes appearance, operation or measured performance. For jurisdiction/platform obligations, inspect current official guidance for the actual destination and claim; do not issue blanket clearance from a general rule. User approval and aesthetic acceptance cannot substitute for substantiation.
