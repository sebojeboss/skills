# Static layout and compositing

## When to read

Use for complete static ads, package-text repairs, cutouts placed into scenes or editable layout revisions.

## How to do the work

Start with authoritative product photography/art where possible. Choose a background compatible with the available camera view. A cutout preserves product detail but cannot create a truthful unseen angle. Arrange hierarchy at delivery size before spending effort on microscopic retouching: product recognition, message, proof, offer and CTA should be intelligible in the chosen order.

Use an editable SVG master for ordinary type, shapes and placed raster assets. Keep relative local assets and explicit font requirements; avoid remote images/fonts/scripts. Preserve editable text in the master, and create an outlined delivery derivative only when needed for compatibility and permitted by the font license. For complex raster work, retain the user's native layered editor format rather than forcing an SVG conversion. An export is not a master.

Separate the product, label, shadow, background and effects. A mask selects visible regions; **occlusion** is the depth relationship that makes one object appear in front of another. Check contact shadow placement, cast-shadow direction, reflection, edge contamination and scale. Some highlights explain glossy material; removing all bright spots can flatten it. Use local corrections before replacing already correct product detail.

## Worked decisions

Illustrative: a matte jar on a warm tabletop needs a grounded contact shadow and accurate label. Replace an erroneous label with verified art, then adjust its perspective and light integration. If the scene requires the unseen rear of the jar, obtain that view or change the composition instead of warping the front into an impossible shape.

Clear glass is harder: original pixels may contain the old background transmitted through the bottle. Keeping every pixel can preserve the wrong environment. A new photograph or skilled reconstruction may be necessary; a clean silhouette alone does not solve it.

## Deliverable and checks

Deliver master plus linked assets, a review image and the requested export. Inspect mobile-size readability, correct text, crop and package identity after export. Verify links/fonts in a fresh opening. Use an available compatible editor/browser for masks, label warps, preview and a verified export path. If absent, deliver the actual editable layout and precise remaining export requirement rather than claim a finished raster.
